# Scrum Lite – Hackathon Workflow

**Goal:** Efficient development during the hackathon with short meetings, continuous integration and clear responsibilities. In Heilbronn we have about 19–20 working hours: Saturday 10:30 to the final deadline on Sunday 15:45. Milestones are in `docs/mission-scenario.md`.

## 1. Sprint Structure

- **Sprint Duration:** 120 minutes.
- Each sprint has a clearly defined goal.
- Tasks are selected from the Product Backlog.
- Breaks are taken between sprints as needed.

## 2. Sprint Planning (5 min)

**At the beginning of each sprint:**
- Review and prioritize backlog items.
- Define the sprint goal.
- Assign tasks to developers.
- Identify dependencies and blockers.

## 3. Development (90 min)

**During development:**
- Developers work on their assigned tasks.
- Update task statuses on the Kanban board.
- Report blockers immediately.
- Keep communication short and focused.

## 4. Integration & Testing (15 min)

**Before the Sprint Review:**
- Integrate completed features through Pull Requests.
- Verify interfaces and component compatibility.
- Run relevant tests and fix critical integration issues.
- Keep `main` stable and demo-ready.

## 5. Sprint Review & Retrospective (10 min)

**At the end of each sprint:**
- Demonstrate completed features.
- Check whether the sprint goal was achieved.
- Discuss what worked and what needs improvement.
- Move completed tasks to Done.
- Reprioritize unfinished tasks.
